How it works
  • Stage 1, inbound: your message is checked for prompt-injection tricks.
  • Stage 2, outbound: the same text is checked as if the bot tried to post it: secrets, banned hype or promises, unknown links, length.
  • Review: claims like partnerships or listings are held for a human instead of posted.
  • "Passed the filter" only means these rules didn't match. On the real bot, the AI also has its own instructions.
